Files in this directory are the unmodified woff2 subsets served by Google
Fonts for the Cairo variable font, covering these unicode ranges:

  cairo-000000.woff2  latin-ext
  cairo-010002.woff2  latin
  cairo-060006.woff2  arabic

No IBM Plex Sans Arabic files are kept. That family appeared second in a
font stack that Cairo already satisfies, so it never rendered, and the
16 files it needed were 411KB of nothing.
